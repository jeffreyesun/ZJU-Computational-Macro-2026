\section{Stages}
% =============================================================

\begin{frame}{Stages}%CLAUDE Note: Never translate "Stage" into Chinese. Keep it as "Stage." Also always capitalize "Stage."
  \begin{columns}[T]
  \begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
  The primary purpose of this course is to introduce you to solving heterogeneous-agent macro models using \textbf{Stages}.

  \textbf{Stages} are the basic building blocks of heterogeneous-agent macro models. They define household problems, which are the \textbf{hard part}.

  Because \textbf{Stages} are conceptually \textbf{well-defined}, \textbf{modular}, and \textbf{composable}, you can build complex macro models by simply combining \textbf{Stages}.
  \end{column}
  \hfill
  \begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
  本课程的主要目的，是教你用 \textbf{Stage} 来求解异质性主体宏观模型。

  \textbf{Stage} 是异质性主体宏观模型的基本构件。它们定义家庭问题——也就是建模中\textbf{最难的部分}。

  因为 \textbf{Stage} 在概念上\textbf{定义清晰}、\textbf{模块化}、\textbf{可组合}，只需把若干 \textbf{Stage} 拼起来，就能搭出复杂的宏观模型。
  \end{column}
  \end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Stages Solve the Household Block \\ Stage 解决家庭模块}
  \begin{columns}[T]
  \begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
  A \textbf{Stage} defines how households make decisions given their \textbf{environment}: \textbf{prices} and \textbf{parameters}.

  We can combine \textbf{Stages} to solve the \textbf{household block}: Value functions and populations given prices and parameters.

  This allows us to solve for \textbf{aggregates}, such as:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Goods demand
    \item Capital demand
    \item Labour supply
  \end{itemize}
  \end{column}
  \hfill
  \begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
  一个 \textbf{Stage} 定义了在给定\textbf{环境}（\textbf{价格}与\textbf{参数}）下，家庭如何作决策。

  把若干 \textbf{Stage} 组合起来，就能解出\textbf{家庭模块}：在给定价格与参数时的价值函数与人口分布。

  由此即可求出各类\textbf{总量}，例如：
  \begin{itemize}
    \item 商品需求
    \item 资本需求
    \item 劳动供给
  \end{itemize}
  \end{column}
  \end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Everything Else is \textbf{Outer Loops} \\ 其余的一切都是\textbf{外层循环}}
  \begin{columns}[T]
  \begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
  If we solve the \textbf{household block} using \textbf{Stages}, everything else is \textbf{Outer Loops}.

  Equilibrium: Solve \textbf{market clearing condition} in an outer loop, together with \textbf{firm} or \textbf{government} blocks.

  Steady State: Iterate population $\lambda$ forward.

  Transition: Solve for a \textbf{path} of market-clearing prices.

  Global Solution with Aggregate Uncertainty: Solve for value function conditional on aggregate state.
  \end{column}
  \hfill
  \begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
  当我们用 \textbf{Stage} 解了\textbf{家庭模块}之后，余下的一切都是\textbf{外层循环}。

  均衡：在外层循环里求解\textbf{市场出清条件}，并与\textbf{企业}或\textbf{政府}模块联立。

  稳态：把人口 $\lambda$ 向前迭代。

  过渡：求出市场出清价格的\textbf{一条路径}。

  含聚合不确定性的全局解：在给定聚合状态下求价值函数。
  \end{column}
  \end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Why Stages? \\ 为何要用 Stage？}
  \begin{columns}[T]
  \begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
  Stages are \textbf{composable}: You can build complex models \textbf{simply} by combining Stages.

  Stages have \textbf{precise mathematical definitions}: You can define and solve a model without worrying about \textbf{implementation details}.

  Stages are \textbf{standardized}: You can \textbf{download} optimized implementations of Stages, and use them to build \textbf{your models}. (e.g. My HouseholdStages.jl, the SSJ Toolkit, or ECON-ARK).
  \end{column}
  \hfill
  \begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
  Stage 是\textbf{可组合的}：把若干 Stage 拼起来，就能\textbf{轻易地}搭出复杂模型。

  Stage 有\textbf{精确的数学定义}：在定义并求解模型时，无需担心\textbf{实现细节}。

  Stage 是\textbf{标准化的}：你可以\textbf{下载}经过优化的 Stage 实现，用它们来搭建\textbf{自己的模型}。 (e.g. 我的 HouseholdStages.jl, SSJ Toolkit, 或者 ECON-ARK)
  \end{column}
  \end{columns}
\end{frame}

\section{Aggregates / 总量}
% =============================================================

\begin{frame}{Read $\bar K$, $\bar L$, $\bar C$ off $\lambda^\star$ \\ 从 $\lambda^\star$ 读出 $\bar K$、$\bar L$、$\bar C$}
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
Every aggregate is an inner product against $\lambda^\star$:
\begin{align*}
  \bar K &= \sum_{i,j} b_i \, \lambda^\star_{ij} \\
  \bar L &= \sum_{i,j} \lambda^\star_{ij}        \\
  \bar C &= \sum_{i,j} c^\star_{ij} \, \lambda^\star_{ij} \\
  \bar V &= \sum_{i,j} V_{ij} \, \lambda^\star_{ij}
\end{align*}

\vspace{0.4em}

Labour supply $\bar L$ is just the (un-normalized) population mass --- exogenous.

\vspace{0.4em}

Capital $\bar K$, consumption $\bar C$, and welfare $\bar V$ all move with $R$.
\end{column}
\hfill
\begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
每个总量都是与 $\lambda^\star$ 的内积：
\begin{align*}
  \bar K &= \sum_{i,j} b_i \, \lambda^\star_{ij} \\
  \bar L &= \sum_{i,j} \lambda^\star_{ij}        \\
  \bar C &= \sum_{i,j} c^\star_{ij} \, \lambda^\star_{ij} \\
  \bar V &= \sum_{i,j} V_{ij} \, \lambda^\star_{ij}
\end{align*}

\vspace{0.4em}

劳动供给 $\bar L$ 就是（未归一化的）人口总质量——外生。

\vspace{0.4em}

资本 $\bar K$、消费 $\bar C$、福利 $\bar V$ 都随 $R$ 变动。
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]{\texttt{compute\_household\_aggregates} \\ 计算家庭总量}
\begin{lstlisting}
function compute_household_aggregates(;param_vals...)
    params = get_params(;param_vals...)
    (V_end, λ) = solve_aiyagari_steady_state(params)

    b_grid_policy = policy_function(V_end, params)
    c_grid_policy = get_c_policy(params.b_grid, b_grid_policy)

    L_agg = sum(λ)
    K_agg = sum(params.b_grid .* λ)
    V_agg = sum(V_end .* λ)
    C_agg = sum(c_grid_policy .* λ)

    return (; V_end, λ, L_agg, K_agg, V_agg, C_agg)
end
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
Take parameter overrides (notably $R$), solve the household block, read off the four aggregates.

\vspace{0.4em}

The entire demand side, in one function.
\end{column}
\hfill
\begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
接收参数覆盖（重点是 $R$），求解家庭模块，再读出四个总量。

\vspace{0.4em}

整个需求侧，就这一个函数。
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Cobb--Douglas \\ Cobb--Douglas 生产函数}
  \begin{columns}[T]
  \begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
  A representative firm with Cobb--Douglas production:
  \[
    Y = K^\alpha L^{1-\alpha}.
  \]

  \vspace{0.4em}

  Given aggregate $K$ and $L$ from the household block, output $Y$ is a one-liner.

  \vspace{0.4em}

  No firm dynamics, no entry, no heterogeneity --- just a production function.

  \vspace{0.4em}

  The entire supply side is one line of code.
  \end{column}
  \hfill
  \begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
  代表性企业，Cobb--Douglas 生产函数：
  \[
    Y = K^\alpha L^{1-\alpha}.
  \]

  \vspace{0.4em}

  给定家庭模块给出的总量 $K$ 与 $L$，产出 $Y$ 一行代码就算出来。

  \vspace{0.4em}

  没有企业动态，没有进入退出，没有异质性——就是一个生产函数。

  \vspace{0.4em}

  整个供给侧只有一行代码。
  \end{column}
  \end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]{\texttt{compute\_Y} \\ 计算产出}
\begin{lstlisting}
compute_Y(K, L; α=1/3) = K^α * L^(1-α)
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
That's it. Pass the household block's $\bar K$ and $\bar L$ in, get $Y$ out.
\end{column}
\hfill
\begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
就这么简单。把家庭模块的 $\bar K$ 与 $\bar L$ 传进来，就得到 $Y$。
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

\section{Equilibrium / 均衡}
% =============================================================

\begin{frame}{Goods market clearing \\ 商品市场出清}
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
In steady state, what households consume must equal what the firm produces:
\[
  \bar C \;=\; Y.
\]

\vspace{0.4em}

Define the \emph{excess demand} for goods:
\[
  \text{excess\_demand}(R) \;:=\; \frac{\bar C(R) - Y(R)}{Y(R)}.
\]

\vspace{0.4em}

Equilibrium is the $R$ that makes $\text{excess\_demand}(R) = 0$.
\end{column}
\hfill
\begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
稳态下，家庭消费必须等于企业产出：
\[
  \bar C \;=\; Y.
\]

\vspace{0.4em}

定义\emph{超额需求}：
\[
  \text{excess\_demand}(R) \;:=\; \frac{\bar C(R) - Y(R)}{Y(R)}.
\]

\vspace{0.4em}

均衡即满足 $\text{excess\_demand}(R) = 0$ 的 $R$。
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Why $\bar C$ and $Y$ both depend on $R$ \\ 为何 $\bar C$ 与 $Y$ 都依赖 $R$}
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
$R$ flows through the household block:
\[
  R \;\to\; V, c^\star \;\to\; \lambda^\star \;\to\; (\bar K, \bar C).
\]

\vspace{0.4em}

Then through the firm:
\[
  (\bar K, \bar L) \;\to\; Y.
\]

\vspace{0.4em}

So one $R$ pins down one $(\bar C, Y)$ pair. Move $R$ until they agree.

\vspace{0.4em}

This loop is exactly the \textbf{Outer Loop} we promised on day one.
\end{column}
\hfill
\begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
$R$ 沿家庭模块传递：
\[
  R \;\to\; V, c^\star \;\to\; \lambda^\star \;\to\; (\bar K, \bar C).
\]

\vspace{0.4em}

再交给企业：
\[
  (\bar K, \bar L) \;\to\; Y.
\]

\vspace{0.4em}

所以每个 $R$ 唯一确定一对 $(\bar C, Y)$。挪动 $R$ 直到二者一致。

\vspace{0.4em}

这个循环正是第一天承诺的\textbf{外层循环}。
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]{\texttt{compute\_excess\_demand} \\ 超额需求}
\begin{lstlisting}
function compute_excess_demand(R)
    res = compute_household_aggregates(;R)
    Y_agg = compute_Y(res.K_agg, res.L_agg)
    return excess_demand = (res.C_agg - Y_agg) / Y_agg
end
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
Solve the household block at this $R$, plug $(\bar K, \bar L)$ into the production function, return the relative gap between $\bar C$ and $Y$.

\vspace{0.4em}

The household block (Stages) and the firm (one line) are glued together by this little wrapper.
\end{column}
\hfill
\begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
在给定 $R$ 下解家庭模块，把 $(\bar K, \bar L)$ 代入生产函数，再返回 $\bar C$ 与 $Y$ 的相对差距。

\vspace{0.4em}

家庭模块（Stage）与企业（一行）就靠这个小包装函数粘在一起。
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]{Solve for the equilibrium $R$ \\ 求解均衡 $R$}
\begin{lstlisting}
tol = 0.0001
lr  = 0.01

R   = 1.03
err = Inf
while err > tol
    excess_demand = compute_excess_demand(R)
    R = R + excess_demand * lr
    println("R=$R\t excess_demand=$excess_demand")
end
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
A simple gradient-style update: nudge $R$ in the direction of excess goods demand.

\vspace{0.4em}

If $\bar C > Y$, households are consuming more than the firm produces --- raise $R$ to encourage saving, lift $K$ and hence $Y$.

\vspace{0.4em}

If $\bar C < Y$, households are saving too much --- lower $R$.
\end{column}
\hfill
\begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
一个简单的梯度式更新：按超额需求的符号微调 $R$。

\vspace{0.4em}

若 $\bar C > Y$，家庭消费多于企业产出——提高 $R$ 鼓励储蓄，使 $K$（进而 $Y$）变大。

\vspace{0.4em}

若 $\bar C < Y$，家庭储蓄过多——降低 $R$。
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
A complete Aiyagari model:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Household block: three \textbf{Stages} from L3, reused unchanged.
  \item Firm: one line of code.
  \item Equilibrium: $\bar C = Y$.
  \item An \textbf{Outer Loop} on $R$ to enforce it.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.6em}

Stages do the demand side. The outer loop closes the model.

\vspace{0.4em}

Notice the asymmetry: the hard part (household block) is the part we get for free.
\end{column}
\hfill
\begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
一个完整的 Aiyagari 模型：
\begin{itemize}
  \item 家庭模块：第三讲的三个 \textbf{Stage}，原封不动。
  \item 企业：一行代码。
  \item 均衡：$\bar C = Y$。
  \item 在 $R$ 上的\textbf{外层循环}保证均衡成立。
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.6em}

Stage 负责需求侧；外层循环把模型闭合。
